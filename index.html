Lesson 1-6 · Convert Customary Measurement Units

Fractions are fine: type 1/2 or 33 1/3. Decimals too: 1.5
Customary Conversions
Type of MeasureLarger Unit→Smaller Unit
Length 1 foot (ft)=12 inches (in.)
1 yard (yd)=3 feet
1 mile (mi)=5,280 feet
Weight 1 pound (lb)=16 ounces (oz)
1 ton (T)=2,000 pounds
Capacity 1 cup (c)=8 fluid ounces (fl oz)
1 pint (pt)=2 cups
1 quart (qt)=2 pints
1 gallon (gal)=4 quarts
